Who we are

A registered non-profit, run on trust.

Founded in 2020 and based in Chhattisgarh, InAmigos Foundation operates across India through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers — with the credentials to back every claim.

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organisation committed to creating lasting social impact. With its base in Chhattisgarh, the foundation operates across India, addressing critical societal issues through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers.

From distributing meals to training interns, from planting saplings to feeding stray animals, our work is grounded in one idea: lasting social change happens when communities, volunteers, and organisations act together, consistently, over time.

Our transparent operations and strong partnerships enable us to bring real, measurable change to society. Through our social media presence and online platforms, we ensure that every initiative reaches the right audience, encouraging more individuals and organisations to join our mission.

Whether through volunteering, partnerships, or donations, every contribution strengthens our cause and enables us to expand our reach and impact. Together, we can build a more inclusive, compassionate, and empowered society.

Founder & CEO

Mr. Govind Shukla

Leads InAmigos Foundation's mission from its base in Sipat, Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, directing a growing volunteer and intern network across the country.

Since 2020, that network has grown to include six active initiatives spanning food security, education, animal welfare, women's empowerment, environment, and youth employment.
