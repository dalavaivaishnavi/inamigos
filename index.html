Who we are

Built on trust, run by volunteers.

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 and is based in Chhattisgarh, operating projects across India through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers. It is a fully registered Section 8 non-profit, keeping every rupee and every hour of volunteer time channelled through a transparent, accountable structure.

From distributing meals to training interns, from planting saplings to feeding stray animals, our work is grounded in one idea: lasting social change happens when communities, volunteers, and organisations act together, consistently, over time.
