Our initiatives

Six projects, one mission.

Each initiative carries a Hindi name that describes exactly what it does — service, education, life, ascent, nature, and growth. Together they form InAmigos Foundation's on-ground work across India.

स

Project SEVA

Service

Distributing meals and clothing to underprivileged families across our project areas — because food and dignity should never be optional.

50,000+ meals & clothing items distributed
ब

Project BACHPANSHALA

School of childhood

Bridging educational gaps by training underprivileged children in basic digital literacy, life skills, and school education support.

Ongoing digital & life-skills classes
जी

Project JEEV

Living being

Caring for animal welfare through daily feeding drives, run entirely by our volunteer network across project locations.

50+ stray animals fed daily
उ

Project UDAAN

Flight / rise

Empowering women by partnering with rural self-help groups to promote financial independence, skill development, and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Rural self-help group partnerships
प्र

Project PRAKRITI

Nature

Advocating for sustainability and environmental conservation through sapling plantation drives and support for eco-friendly agriculture.

20,000+ saplings planted
वि

Project VIKAS

Development

Facilitating employment and skill-building through internships in data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing, social work, and more.

30,000+ interns trained in four years
