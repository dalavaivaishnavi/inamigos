Get involved

Volunteer with us.

Every one of our six initiatives runs on volunteer time and effort. Here's what's involved, and what you get in return for showing up.

Why volunteer

What volunteers get.

Drive schedules

Early notice on meal distributions, plantation drives, and animal feeding rounds near you.

Pick your cause

Choose from all six initiatives — education, environment, women's empowerment, and more.

Internship access

Volunteers get priority consideration for Project VIKAS internships.

Certificate of service

A record of your hours and contributions, useful for résumés and school/college credit.

How it works

Getting started is simple.

1. Reach out

Send us a message through our contact page with the initiative you're interested in.

2. Hear back

Our volunteer coordinator in Bilaspur will get in touch with the next drive or opening.

3. Show up

Join us on the ground — at a distribution drive, a plantation, a classroom, or a shelter.

4. Stay involved

Many of our volunteers return again and again, and some go on to intern with Project VIKAS.

Ready to lend a hand?

Get in touch and tell us which initiative you'd like to support — we'll take it from there.
